==========================================
        My Open Source Manifesto
        Date: 30-March-2026
==========================================

Every day I rely on Kali Linux, a tool built by the open-source community,
which reminds me that true power comes from sharing knowledge freely.
To me, 'Trust' embodies the spirit of collaboration and innovation.
I aspire to build and share Program files openly, contributing to a world where technology
is accessible to all, and where ideas can grow beyond individual ownership.

Open source is not just software; it is a philosophy of collective growth.
By sharing our creations, we ensure that everyone benefits and learns.
==========================================
